Frequently asked questions

Can I eat at the restaurant?

Sorry, no. We're a takeaway-only kitchen and don't have a seating area. Every order is packed to take away.

Do you deliver?

We don't run our own delivery. Order here for collection and pick it up from our counter.

How do I pay?

You pay when you collect, by cash or card. There's nothing to pay online.

How long will my order take?

Most orders are ready in 15–25 minutes. Large or family orders can take a little longer at busy times. You can also schedule a later pickup slot.

Is your food halal?

Yes, all our meat is 100% halal.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Call us on 0300 1234567 as soon as possible with your order number and we'll do our best to help.
